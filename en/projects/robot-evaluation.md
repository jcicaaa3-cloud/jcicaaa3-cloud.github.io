A research project on the criteria for judging a robot task as a success or a failure, done in the spring 2026 semester.

When a robot finishes its motion but doesn't achieve the goal, whether that counts as success or failure depends on the criterion. You need to settle that before comparing success rates, so I made the criterion itself the research topic.

- 80 runs, 874 analyzed cases
- Telling motion completion apart from goal achievement
- How the verdict on the same action changes when the criterion changes
- Handling cases that are hard to call either way
