## Research question

When we classify the outcomes of robot actions as successes or failures, what exactly should those labels mean? This study examines how different criteria affect the evaluation of a robotic task, particularly when completion of a motion and achievement of the goal do not align.

## Experimental record

- **Execution runs:** 80
- **Analyzed cases:** 874
- **Focus:** Decision criteria, variation in evaluation outcomes, and ambiguous cases

## Questions examined

- How should motion completion be distinguished from task completion?
- How does changing the criterion alter the label assigned to the same action?
- How should borderline cases be treated when a binary success/failure judgment is inadequate?

Before comparing success rates, it is necessary to define **what counts as success**. The evaluation criterion itself is therefore the subject of this study.
