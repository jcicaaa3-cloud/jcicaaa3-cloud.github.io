My graduation research on what criteria to use when splitting robot task outcomes into success and failure. I'm still writing it up.

## Overview

Sometimes a robot finishes its motion but doesn't achieve the goal. When finishing the motion and achieving the goal don't line up, the verdict depends on which criterion you use. To compare success rates you first have to decide what counts as success, so I made the criterion itself the subject of the study.

## Experiments

80 runs and 874 analyzed cases. The focus was on the criteria, how results differ between them, and cases that are hard to call.

## Questions

- How do you tell finishing a task apart from achieving its goal?
- How does the result for the same action change when the criterion changes?
- What do you do with cases that don't clearly fit either success or failure?
